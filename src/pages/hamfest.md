## Free Hamfest

**Hidden Valleys Amateur Radio Club Hamfest**  
Lancaster, Wisconsin

### Date & Time

**Saturday, October 16, 2027**  
**8:00 AM to 1:00 PM**

### Location

**Grant County Fairgrounds**  
Youth and Ag Building  
916 East Elm Street (County Road A)  
Lancaster, Wisconsin 53813

### VE Testing

VE testing for new license candidates takes place at 10:00 AM.

### Talk-in

146.52 MHz

### Details

#### Free drawing for door prizes

- Free parking
- Free table for sellers
- Free admission
- Free coffee and donuts
- Refreshments and lunch available
