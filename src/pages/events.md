## Winter Field Day

This event is held annually on the last Saturday of January. It is an event organized by the [Winter Field Day Association](https://winterfieldday.org/). It consists of activities conducted by amateur radio operators, including a radio contest, demonstrations for the public, media outreach, and emergency communications practice.

HVARC occasionally participates in Winter Field Day. The event location is decided at meetings beforehand. Equipment is supplied by operators, and the club owns a trailer used for operations. The club uses its call sign (KC9KQ) so new and inexperienced operators can participate in contacts reserved for General and Amateur Extra licensees.

## Wisconsin QSO Party

The West Allis Radio Amateur Club hosts this event annually on the second weekend of March. HVARC members participate every year. While the ultimate goal is to activate all 72 counties, club members tend to use their own discretion in participating in the event. It takes place on the second Sunday of March. Be ready because that's also the first day of DST in the USA for the year! However, one can observe the history of Wisconsin QSO Party at the event website

More information is available on [WARAC's site](https://www.warac.org/wqp/wqp.htm).
