## Field Day

This event is held annually on the last Saturday of June. It is an event organized by the American Radio Relay League. It consists of activities conducted by amateur radio operators, including a radio contest, demonstrations for the public, media outreach, and emergency communications practice.

HVARC regularly participates in Field Day. The event location is decided at meetings beforehand. Equipment is supplied by operators, and the club owns a trailer used for operations. The club uses its call sign (KC9KQ) so new and inexperienced operators can participate in contacts reserved for General and Amateur Extra licensees.

## Wisconsin QSO Party

The West Allis Radio Amateur Club hosts this event annually on the second weekend of March. HVARC members participate every year. More information is available on [WARAC's site](https://www.warac.org/wqp/wqp.htm).
