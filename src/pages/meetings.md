## In Person

The Hidden Valleys Amateur Radio Club meets on the second Tuesday of each month at Southwest Health in Platteville, Wisconsin. Meetings are at 7:00 PM, and anyone interested in amateur radio, licensed or not, is welcome.

- July's meeting is the summer picnic, usually held at the Platteville Museum unless otherwise announced.
- December's meeting is replaced by the annual Holiday Party. The location and date are decided one month beforehand.

## On-the-Air Nets

see [Repeater sections in Amateur Radio](./amateur-radio.md#beetown-repeater-n0wlu) for tuning paramters for the repeaters

Sunday evenings, members meet at 7:30 PM on the Beetown (N0WLU) repeater at 146.895 MHz with a PL tone of 114.8. All licensed amateur radio operators are welcome to check in.

Monday and Friday mornings, members meet at 7:00 AM on the same Beetown repeater. These nets are also open to all licensed amateur radio operators.

Tuesday evenings (all Tuesdays of the month except the 2nd - which is our meeting night), Newer members are invited to join us on at 7:30 PM the same repeater. This "Newbie" net is geared to help new hams ease in to being on the air.

## Notes

Meeting dates and times are subject to change without notice.
