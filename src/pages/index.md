Welcome to the home of the Hidden Valleys Amateur Radio Club (HVARC). We are a small, friendly club located in the hills and valleys of southwestern Wisconsin. Our members participate in Field Day, Wisconsin QSO parties, local community service activities, and offer amateur radio licensing classes. We also participate in regular network meetings (nets) on the air. Many members are trained storm spotters; some have CPR training, and a few hold multiple Red Cross certifications. Recently members have invested time in LoRa data communication using MeshCore and Meshtastic.

All amateur radio operators and anyone interested in amateur radio are welcome at club meetings and events.
