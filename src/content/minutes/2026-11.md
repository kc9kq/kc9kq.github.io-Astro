The November meeting of HVARC (Hidden Valleys Amateur Radio Club) was held on November 10th at the Platteville Hospital at 7pm.

There were 13 in attendance (WD9CPL, KC4QFE, WX4RAY, WU9O, KC9BGA, KD9GBJ, K0RMS, KE9DDM, KC9CSQ, KC9MCC, N0WLU, KE9GLL, KE9GLK).

The meeting was called to order at 7:00pm.

The previous meeting's minutes were approved.

The club Treasurer, Scott Onsen, reported a bank balance of $4920.16. $189 in donations came in at the hamfest.

The Christmas party email went out. Forward to others who may wish to attend. The Dubuque club was invited to our Christmas party, but their own Christmas party is the following day, so we might not see many of them attend.

Winter Field Day was discussed, possibly at the PDC Welcome Center, on the last weekend of January.

Election of officers — everything stayed as is, except Mike Dalecki and Justin Harding are now sharing the Secretary role. President: Chuck Richardson. Vice President: Larry Pink. Treasurer: Scott Onsen. D1: Mark Krolick. D2: Duane Stephenson. D3: Keith Amdahl.

Mike Dalecki has some study guides available for printing. We may have some printed up as needed. We will decide in the future.

Robert Smith gave a presentation on AllStarLink.

There is a now a newbie net on the repeater on Tuesday nights hosted by Mike Dalecki.

Also, the hamfest location is reserved for next year at the same place, but not yet paid.

The meeting was adjourned at 8:05pm.
